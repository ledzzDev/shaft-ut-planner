Live scan configuration

Drag the slider to move the probe. In axial mode it scrubs the pulse depth from the entry face; in radial mode it moves the probe physically along the OD, showing the local wall depth as it changes with each section. In axial mode you can also grab the blue probe block in the diagram and drag it to the other end face.

Backup & restore

Everything is saved automatically in this browser, but a Home Screen app on iOS can have its storage cleared by the system after long periods of disuse. Export a backup now and then, and keep the text somewhere safe (Notes, email to yourself, etc).
